## Open Gaps Noticed

1. **Agricultural sector shocks** — Bangladesh is highly vulnerable to crop losses from floods/cyclones but there is no standalone event for a major food security or rice price shock (e.g. 2007–2008 global food crisis impact on Bangladesh). Could be added in a future pass.

2. **Financial sector / banking crisis** — The 2010s saw recurring non-performing loan crises at state-owned banks (Sonali, Janata, BASIC) and several major loan scandals (Hallmark, BASIC Bank). These were material for macroeconomic indicators but did not coalesce into a single discrete event suitable for the current schema.

3. **Education milestones** — Bangladesh achieved near-universal primary enrolment and gender parity in secondary education by the early 2000s; these are important HDI drivers not currently represented.

4. **Natural gas sector** — Offshore gas discoveries and the Bibiyana gas field expansion in the 2000s are potentially relevant for energy-indicator patterns but lack a clean single-year peak event.

5. **2024 post-Hasina political transition details** — The interim government's reform agenda (constitutional commission, electoral reform) was still in progress as of April 2026; a future update should add a 2025 event if a discrete constitutional milestone is reached.

6. **1992–1993 fatwa wave against women** — A documented wave of conservative religious edicts against NGO women's programmes and Taslima Nasrin's writings; could be relevant to religion-and-society indicators if those are added to the catalog.
