## Read

1. **Discrimination is cohort-conditional, and the direction differs by trend.** `military_coup` (0.703) and `territorial_disputes` (0.757) work in fragile states; `banking_and_financial_crisis` works in stable democracies (0.753, n_pos 40) and is at chance in fragile states' tiny cell. A single fleet-level AUC hides this entirely.
2. **mid-tier is the weakest cohort in 5 of 5 recipes** (0.530–0.633) — the hard middle, where a country is neither institutionally stable enough to be predictable nor stressed enough to show a clear pre-event signature.
3. **Small cells are noisy and must not be over-read**: banking fragile-state n=20; military_coup stable-democracy n_pos=4 (0 caught at 0.5). Cohort AUCs on <10 positives are reported for completeness, not for inference.
4. `civil_war_and_insurgency` and `executive_power_consolidation` are comparatively flat across cohorts (0.596–0.674 and 0.633–0.692) — cohort-conditioning is a per-recipe property, not a fleet-wide constant.
